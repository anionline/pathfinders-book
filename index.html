About the Book

Lead the Drop.
Light the Zone.

Blending gripping stories from World War II Pathfinder paratroopers with business case studies, Ani Chaudhuri redefines what it means to build under uncertainty.

From the darkness over Normandy to the chaos of new products, he shows how clarity, courage, and conviction separate ordinary teams from extraordinary ones.

The book connects battlefield discipline to product craftsmanship, revealing that success isn't about resources but about mindset: owning the outcome, simplifying the mission, and lighting the way for others.

The Author

Ani
Chaudhuri

A four-time founder and product builder, Ani most recently co-founded data-security company Dasera, acquired by Netskope in 2024 (Nasdaq: NTSK).

Previously, he built products and services at McKinsey, Tata Steel, and HP. Across startups and global enterprises, his work centers on four unchanging principles: intimate knowledge of your customer, understanding of your customer's problem, differentiation of your solution, and simplicity of your messaging.

Ani lives in California with his wife, two sons, and their dog.
